Portfolio

What we've built with other brands.

From branding to automation systems, here's a look at real projects we've delivered for brands in Venezuela and around the world.

Branding

Identity & graphic design

Incon Container Wholesale

BrandingGraphic Design

Incon delivers demountable, recycled shipping containers anywhere in the world to build homes, businesses and storage spaces. We gave the brand a modular identity where the logo pieces interlock, a direct nod to the product itself, with a high-contrast palette built to stand out on billboards and outdoor advertising.

Baggely Bakery

BrandingGraphic Design

Baggely is a bakery and cafe focused on products free from common allergens. We built a sweet, youthful and approachable brand that shows eating differently doesn't mean giving anything up.

X-Build

BrandingGraphic DesignSocial MediaAds

X-Build is an Australian startup developing 3D concrete printing technology for housing. The challenge was positioning it as a cutting-edge tech company inside a historically conservative industry. Bluz now handles its visual identity, social media, paid ads and lead generation.

Vibrant Architecture Studio

BrandingGraphic Design

Vibrant is a studio specialized in parametric architecture. We designed a dynamic identity that mirrors how they work: algorithms and parameters turned into spaces that break from convention.

Content and Social Media

Photography, editing & direction

Rooties

A Venezuelan propolis spray brand created by moms. We manage their social media, handling photography, content and creative direction for their channels, showing the product in real, everyday moments.

MioLunetto

An Argentine medialunas bakery and cafe. We create content that mixes everyday humor with high-quality product photography.

Compremos En China

A China-to-Venezuela logistics and import company. We manage their social media: designing content, writing reel scripts and producing educational pieces that break down a complex process, importing a vehicle, into something simple to understand.

Systems and Automation

CRM, automation & lead scoring

Locker creation system

Compremos En China

We designed an automated system that connects Wix, Meta, Make and Google Apps Script. When a customer fills out their registration form, the system generates their locker code, creates the shipping paperwork, emails them everything they need and confirms it all over WhatsApp, no manual work required.

Lead scoring system

Flatpack Container

We built a lead scoring system that automatically classifies each prospect by customer type, unit volume, estimated budget and financing needs. It segments contacts, assigns tasks to the sales team and sends events back to Meta to optimize campaigns.

Web

Corporate websites

X-Build & Compremos En China

We built the corporate websites for both brands, designed to turn visits into qualified leads, with contact forms tied directly into their CRM systems.
