This is a **sample draft**. It shows how Bluz Lab articles read: headings, paragraphs, lists and quotes. It isn't published while it has `draft: true`.

## How to write a new article

1. Copy this file into `src/content/lab/en/` with a new name.
2. Change `slug`, `title`, `description` and `date`.
3. Write the content in Markdown.
4. Remove `draft: true` to publish it.

> A good Bluz Lab article answers a concrete question a client asked us this week.
